### Recurring Themes Across Reports (for your own awareness — not a coding checklist item)
- **Linked list "remove Nth/Kth from end"** has now been independently reported across three different dates (Mar 31, Apr 19, plus your own actual interview) — strong, repeated signal.
- **HM/behavioral rounds** consistently use STAR-format prompts: leaving current company, why eBay, helping a peer, biggest impact, conflict handling, feedback received. Worth having 2-3 solid stories ready that can flex across these prompts.
- **eBay does pool hiring** per one report — you may not know your final team even after rounds are complete; not something you can prep around, just useful context.
- **System design rounds vary widely by interviewer** — from "design Dropbox" (classic) to "you can't use any existing DB, build your own storage reasoning" (harder, less standard) — so rehearsing rigid memorized designs is less valuable than practicing the reasoning/tradeoff-articulation muscle itself.
